About

I'm an AI and data engineer based in Paris, working freelance on AI automation. I build RAG agents, n8n automations, computer vision models and the data models and pipelines underneath them.

Since January 2026 I have been the data engineer at Ghazeline, a ready-to-wear brand, where I designed the PostgreSQL / Supabase data model for its online store, maintain it, and deployed an n8n AI agent that automates its Instagram replies. Before that I was an ML engineer on RideOnTrack's railway anomaly detection project for Infrabel, and a generative AI engineer at Algorhythm, where I built a RAG agent over BigQuery data inside HubSpot.
