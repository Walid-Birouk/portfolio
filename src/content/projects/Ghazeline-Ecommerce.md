## Data Model and AI Agent for a Fashion E-commerce Store

**Role:** Data & AI Engineer (freelance) · **Client:** Ghazeline, a women's fashion brand · **Since:** January 2026

## Overview

Ghazeline sells ready-to-wear fashion through its online store and Instagram. I joined as a freelance data engineer to give the store a structured data foundation, automate the repetitive parts of order handling, and take routine customer questions off the team's hands.

## What I Built

- **Data model (PostgreSQL / Supabase)** — products, variants, categories, stock and orders for a catalogue of 30+ products.
- **n8n AI agent for Instagram** — answers the store's 50–100 daily customer messages; the team only steps in for complex requests.
- **Order automation** — automated order summaries and cash-on-delivery ordering with phone confirmation, removing a manual step from every order.
- **Backend data collection** — features that feed the data model from the store's operations.

## Ongoing Work

I maintain the store in production: fixes and new features driven by business feedback, and monitoring of the data flows.

## Key Technologies

- **PostgreSQL / Supabase** — operational database and backend
- **n8n** — workflow automation and the AI agent
